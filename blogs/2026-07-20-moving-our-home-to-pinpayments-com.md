Just recently we relocated the Pin Payments website from pin.net.au to pinpayments.com. We’re now helping small businesses in New Zealand accept card payments, and are exploring other locations outside of Australia/NZ where similar businesses would benefit from having access to our product. Moving to pinpayments.com is one of the steps as part of that journey.
