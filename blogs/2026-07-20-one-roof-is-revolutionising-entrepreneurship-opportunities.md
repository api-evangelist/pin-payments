For years, One Roof has been a place where aspiring women entrepreneurs had the opportunities and resources needed to turn their ideas into reality. Co-founded by corporate lawyer turned entrepreneur Sheree Rubinstein, One Roof was set up in 2016. As they grew more prominent over the years, One Roof quickly became the leading co-working space for women in Australia.
