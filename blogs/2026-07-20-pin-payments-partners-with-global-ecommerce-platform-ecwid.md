Pin Payments , an Australian payment solutions provider, today announced a partnership with global ecommerce platform Ecwid by Lightspeed , in an effort to help Australian small businesses compete in the global marketplace. With more than 2 million small businesses in Australia, according to the Australian Small Business and Family Enterprise Ombudsman , COVID-19 has resulted in many businesses needing greater digital financial support. Ecwid by Lightspeed integration with Pin Payments looks to change this by providing the right tools to help small to medium-sized businesses transition online.
