We’re excited to announce that Pin Payments is now available in New Zealand. Small businesses in New Zealand can now accept online payments from their customers with a low fee of 1.9% + 30c for domestic card transactions . Getting started is free; businesses with an NZBN can sign up and quickly begin to accept payments.
