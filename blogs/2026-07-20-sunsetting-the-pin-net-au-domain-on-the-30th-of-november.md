When we started Pin Payments, pin.net.au was our default domain. Since then, we’ve migrated to pinpayments.com but maintained pin.net.au as part of our legacy infrastructure. To streamline our operations, we’ve decided to retire the pin.net.au domain and migrate all our processes to pinpayments.com from 1st of December 2024.
