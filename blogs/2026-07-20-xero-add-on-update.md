Our Xero update allows you to record payment processing fees and payment reversals from your Pin Payments account. Each payment processing fee will be represented in Xero as a spend money transaction, and payment reversals such as refunds and chargebacks will be represented as a credit note. If you have enabled surcharging , you will see the surcharge amount as a receive money transaction.
