The holiday season can be a wonderful time of the year, but it can also be filled with excessiveness and significant waste. Statistics show waste increases by a whopping 30% at Christmas. On top of that, $400M dollars worth of ‘unwanted’ gifts received , reportedly end up in landfills after the holiday period.
